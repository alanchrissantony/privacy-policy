Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

Three Days Tasks ("the App") is provided by Alan Chriss Antony ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use the App.

In short: the App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The tasks you create are stored only on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any information. The App does not require an account and does not gather your name, email address, contacts, location, device identifiers, usage analytics, diagnostics, or crash reports.

The only information the App handles is the tasks you enter yourself. Each task consists of a title, a due date, and an optional completed date. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

2. How We Use Information

Your tasks are used only within the App, to show you what was due and what you completed today, yesterday, and the day before, and to list all of your tasks. The App does not use your information for any other purpose.

3. Where Information Is Stored

Your tasks are stored only on your device, in the App's private storage area. We do not operate any servers, and the App makes no network requests, so your tasks are never uploaded or synchronized anywhere by the App.

Device backups: if you have turned on Android's device backup, Android may include the App's data in your personal backup to your Google account, as it does for most apps. That backup is controlled by you and handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. We have no access to it.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not share, sell, or rent any information. The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking services, and no data is sent to any service provider.

5. Security

Your tasks are kept in the App's private storage, which Android isolates from other apps on your device. Because the App never transmits your data, it is not exposed to network interception or server-side breaches. The overall security of your tasks also depends on how you protect your device, for example with a screen lock. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Retention and Deletion

Your tasks are kept on your device until you delete them. You can delete any individual task in the App by opening it and tapping Delete task. You can remove all of your tasks at once by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App. Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot recover deleted tasks and there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

7. Permissions

The App does not request any permissions that require your approval. It does not access your camera, microphone, photos, files, contacts, location, or notifications. The Android framework the App is built with may list the standard network-access permission, which Android grants automatically, but the App does not use it to send or receive any data.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children under the age of 13. Because the App does not collect any personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children.

9. Your Choices and Rights

All of your information is under your control on your own device. You can view, edit, and delete your tasks at any time within the App, and you can remove all App data by clearing its storage or uninstalling it. Because we do not collect or hold any of your data, there is no data for us to provide, correct, export, or delete upon request.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
